Why we cross bridges

The Vaal River runs more than a thousand kilometres, from its source in Mpumalanga to where it meets the Orange River in the Northern Cape. Along the way it draws a line between provinces: Mpumalanga, Gauteng and North West on one bank, the Free State on the other.

Here in the Vaal, that river is all that separates neighbours. Vereeniging and Vanderbijlpark on one side, Sasolburg on the other, with the townships, towns and farms that grew up around them. Over generations, the water has shaped different towns, cultures and stories.

But a river is also something you cross. Every day, bridges carry people from one bank to the other. Vaal Festival is our bridge: one day where people from every side of the river leave their differences on the shore and meet in one place, to dance, to talk and to meet someone new.

That is why we made it intimate and intentional. Small enough for real conversations, big enough to feel like a celebration.

Cross over. Meet us by the river.

When is the festival, and what time do gates open?

Saturday 12 December 2026. Gates open at 12h00 and the festival runs until 23h00.

Where is it?

The Hills, 20 Minnaar Street, Wonderfontein, Sasolburg. The festival grounds are on the lawn by the Vaal River.

Get directions
Is there an age limit?

Yes. Vaal Festival is strictly 18 and over. No one under 18 will be allowed into the festival.

Do I need to bring my ID?

You need proof that you are 18 or over. A photo of your ID on your phone is enough. You are welcome to bring your physical ID too, to be safe.

Can I bring my own alcohol?

No. Outside alcohol is not allowed into the festival.

Are bottles allowed?

No. Bottles are not allowed into the festival. Bottled water and ice are sold inside, so you can stay cool on the day.

Is there parking?

Yes. There are parking spaces at the venue.
